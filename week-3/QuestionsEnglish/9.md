10. What time do people go out for dinner? :
The typical time people go out for dinner varies by culture, country, and personal habits, but here are some general guidelines:

### **By Region:**
1. **United States & Canada**
   - **Weekdays:** 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM (most common)
   - **Weekends:** 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM (later, especially for social dining)
   - **Early dinner (families, seniors):** 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM
   - **Late dinner (urban areas, nightlife):** 9:00 PM – 10:30 PM

2. **Europe**
   - **Northern Europe (UK, Germany, Scandinavia):** 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM
   - **Southern Europe (Spain, Italy, Greece):**
     - **Spain:** 9:00 PM – 11:00 PM (very late by global standards)
     - **Italy:** 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM
     - **France:** 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM
   - **Eastern Europe:** 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM

3. **Latin America**
   - **Mexico, Argentina, Brazil:** 8:00 PM – 10:30 PM (often later on weekends)
   - **Colombia, Peru:** 7:00 PM – 9:30 PM

4. **Asia**
   - **China, Japan, South Korea:** 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM (business dinners may go later)
   - **India:** 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM (varies by region)
   - **Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia):** 8:00 PM – 10:30 PM (later due to hot climate)

5. **Australia & New Zealand**
   - 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM (similar to the UK)

### **By Occasion:**
- **Family dinners:** Earlier (5:30 PM – 7:00 PM)
- **Date night/romantic dinners:** Later (7:30 PM – 9:00 PM)
- **Business dinners:** Often 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM
- **Fine dining (multi-course meals):** 8:00 PM or later

### **Factors Affecting Dinner Time:**
- **Work schedules** (people dine later in cities with long commutes).
- **Cultural norms** (Mediterranean countries dine much later).
- **Restaurant hours** (some close by 9 PM, while others stay open until midnight).
- **Season** (in summer, people may dine later due to longer daylight).
