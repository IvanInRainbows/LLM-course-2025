9. I will be graduating university. What to wear? :
Congratulations on your upcoming graduation! What you wear depends on the formality of the event, your personal style, and the weather. Here’s a breakdown of options for different scenarios:

### **1. Under the Graduation Gown (Standard Attire)**
Since you’ll be wearing a cap and gown, your outfit underneath should be **comfortable, presentable, and not too bulky** (to avoid looking awkward under the robe). Here are some classic choices:

#### **For Women:**
- **Dress or Skirt + Blouse:**
  - A **knee-length or midi dress** (solid colors or subtle prints) in a breathable fabric (cotton, linen, or lightweight wool).
  - A **pencil skirt + blouse** (tucked in) for a polished look.
  - Avoid **strapless or spaghetti straps** (gowns can slip, and you may need to adjust).
  - **Neutral or school-color shoes** (low heels, flats, or dressy sandals).

- **Pantsuit or Jumpsuit:**
  - A **tailored pantsuit** (blazer + trousers) in a neutral color (black, navy, gray).
  - A **dressy jumpsuit** (if it’s not too bulky under the gown).

- **Shoes:**
  - **Comfortable heels (2-3 inches max)** or **dressy flats** (you’ll be walking a lot).
  - Avoid **stilettos** (they can sink into grass if outdoors).

#### **For Men:**
- **Dress Shirt + Tie + Dress Pants:**
  - A **crisp button-down shirt** (white, light blue, or school colors).
  - A **tie** (optional but classic—match it to your school colors or keep it neutral).
  - **Dress pants** (khaki, gray, or black).
  - **Dress shoes** (oxfords or loafers—no sneakers).

- **Suit (Optional but Sharp):**
  - A **lightweight suit** (navy, gray, or black) if you want a more formal look.
  - Skip the jacket if it’s hot (just wear the dress shirt + tie + pants).

- **Shoes:**
  - **Polished dress shoes** (no scuffs—you’ll be in photos!).

#### **For All Genders:**
- **Avoid:**
  - **Too much jewelry** (it can get caught in the gown).
  - **Overly casual clothes** (jeans, T-shirts, sneakers).
  - **Fabrics that wrinkle easily** (you’ll be sitting for a while).

---

### **2. After the Ceremony (Celebration Outfit)**
If you’re changing for a party or dinner, you can go for something more **festive and personal**:

#### **For Women:**
- **Cocktail Dress** (if it’s a fancy dinner).
- **Jumpsuit + Heels** (chic and comfortable).
- **Midi Skirt + Cute Top + Sandals** (for a casual-celebratory vibe).
- **School Colors** (if you want to show spirit).

#### **For Men:**
- **Blazer + Dark Jeans + Loafers** (smart casual).
- **Polo Shirt + Chinos** (if it’s a laid-back gathering).
- **Full Suit** (if it’s a formal dinner).

#### **For All Genders:**
- **Comfortable but stylish shoes** (you’ve been standing all day!).
- **A light jacket or cardigan** (in case it gets chilly).

---

### **3. Weather Considerations**
- **Hot/Sunny Day:**
  - **Lightweight, breathable fabrics** (linen, cotton).
  - **Avoid heavy layers** (you’ll be in a gown).
  - **Sunscreen** (if outdoors—graduation robes don’t protect from sunburn!).
  - **A small fan or handheld mist spray** (lifesaver for long ceremonies).

- **Cold/Rainy Day:**
  - **Thermal layers** (a thin sweater under your gown).
  - **Tights or leggings** (if wearing a dress/skirt).
  - **Waterproof shoes** (if outdoors).
  - **A stylish coat** (to wear before/after—remove for photos).

---

### **4. Accessories & Extras**
- **Minimal Jewelry** (stud earrings, a simple necklace, or a watch).
- **A Clutch or Small Crossbody Bag** (for essentials—phone, keys, lipstick).
- **Graduation Stole/Cords** (if you earned honors, wear them proudly!).
- **Comfortable Underwear** (you’ll be sitting/standing for hours—no digging straps!).

---
### **5. Pro Tips**
✅ **Try on your gown beforehand** to see how your outfit looks underneath.
✅ **Iron your clothes** (wrinkles show in photos!).
✅ **Bring safety pins** (in case your gown or cap needs adjusting).
✅ **Wear deodorant & blister bandaids** (just in case).
✅ **Smile and enjoy the day!** You’ve earned it.

### **Final Answer:**
**Under the gown:**
- **Women:** Knee-length dress, pencil skirt + blouse, or pantsuit in neutral/school colors + comfortable shoes.
- **Men:** Dress shirt + tie + dress pants (or full suit) + polished shoes.

**After the ceremony:**
- **Women:** Cocktail dress, jumpsuit, or skirt + top.
- **Men:** Blazer + jeans or polo + chinos.

**Key:** **Comfort + polish + school pride!**
